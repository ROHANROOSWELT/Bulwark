01 Overview & The Problem Statement

DeFi Resilience

Across Aave V3's $17.4 Billion TVL, sudden market volatility pushes healthy borrowing positions into undercollateralization (Health Factor < 1.00). When this threshold is breached, third-party searchers trigger a violent liquidation:

  • Catastrophic Collateral Loss: The borrower incurs an instantaneous 5% to 10% liquidation bonus penalty.
  • Yield Dismantlement: Up to 50% of the user's collateral is sliced (close factor), destroying compounding yield and position continuity.
  • MEV Cascade Gas Wars: Predatory bots congest the public mempool, causing cascading price liquidations across secondary collateral pools.

Existing automation consists of either rigid stop-loss bots (which routinely fail during extreme gas spikes) or unconstrained AI agents that make probabilistic decisions during panic—precisely when a model is most prone to hallucination, drift, or adversarial prompt injection.

BULWARK replaces predatory liquidations with a state-bound, autonomous agent backstop economy. Borrowers pre-authorize bounded, adaptive safety grants while their positions are safe. When volatility strikes, the autonomous underwriter calculates an exact closed-form repayment, the deterministic Policy Compiler clamps the execution to immutable caps, and KeeperHub executes the rescue deterministically with zero secret leakage.

Core Operating Thesis
"Agents propose. Policy compiles. KeeperHub executes. Anyone can prove it."

02 System Architecture & 8-Step Lifecycle

End-to-End Flow

BULWARK operates across three distinct structural layers: the Aave V3 Protocol (lending truth), the BULWARK Desk Agent Layer (risk telemetry, counterfactual ladders, clamp compiler), and KeeperHub Infrastructure (Turnkey HSM custody, idempotency keys, private mempool execution).

STEP 1 Chain State Read Guardian reads collateral ($C$) and debt ($D$) directly from Aave V3 Pool via getUserAccountData.
STEP 2 Underwriting & Ladder Computes counterfactual safety ladder, calculates cost-to-safety, and quotes risk-adjusted premium.
STEP 3 RescueGrant Proposal Borrower or desk drafts a state-bound grant with adaptive HF bands, expiry timestamp, and ceiling caps.
STEP 4 Human Owner Approval Position owner explicitly signs/approves the grant via Web UI or CLI. State transitions to ARMED.
STEP 5 Policy Compilation Deterministic Policy Compiler clamps proposed intent to active band ceiling and signs authorityHash.
STEP 6 Zero-Revert Simulation KeeperHub dry-runs the payload with simulate: true, confirming zero execution reverts before broadcast.
STEP 7 Turnkey Idempotent Exec KeeperHub signs transaction via Turnkey enclave and broadcasts to private mempool with unique Idempotency-Key.
STEP 8 Dual Receipt & PoAA Proof Dual-receipt verifier validates KeeperHub and public RPC receipts; exports 11-check cryptographic proof bundle.

07 Mathematical Formulations: Closed-Form Debt Targeting

Exact Analytical Solution

Traditional liquidation bots guess repayment amounts using arbitrary heuristic increments, causing over-repayment or leaving positions dangerously close to re-liquidation. BULWARK calculates the exact closed-form debt repayment ($\Delta D^*$) required to restore an undercollateralized position to a target health factor ($H_{\text{target}} = 1.50$ or $2.00$):

Aave V3 Health Factor Definition
H = (C × L) / D

To reach target health factor $H_{\text{target}}$ by repaying debt amount $\Delta D^*$:

Targeting Condition
H_target = (C × L) / (D - ΔD*)

Solving analytically for $\Delta D^*$ yields the exact formula implemented in packages/core/src/underwriter/desk.ts:

Exact Closed-Form Solution
ΔD* = (D × H_target - C × L) / H_target
  • Zero Capital Extraction Invariant: If $H \ge H_{\text{target}}$, $\Delta D^* = 0$. The desk is mathematically prohibited from touching healthy positions.
  • Debt Exhaustion Invariant: If $\Delta D^* > D$, the amount is clamped to $D$ (full debt repayment).

08 Risk-Adjusted Dynamic Premium Urgency Ladder

Convex Pricing Model

Underwriter risk scales non-linearly as a borrower's Health Factor nears $1.00$. To compensate emergency liquidity providers fairly while remaining significantly cheaper than Aave's 5%–10% liquidation bonus penalty, BULWARK applies a convex urgency pricing curve:

Dynamic Premium Pricing Equation
P(H) = P_base × (1 + κ × ((H_crit - H) / (H_crit - 1.00))^γ)
  • $P_{\text{base}}$: Base underwriting fee (e.g. 50 bps / 0.50%).
  • $H_{\text{crit}}$: Critical trigger floor threshold ($1.25$ HF).
  • $\kappa$: Maximum urgency multiplier ($\kappa = 3.0$).
  • $\gamma$: Convexity acceleration exponent ($\gamma = 1.5$).

When $H = 1.25$, $P(H) = P_{\text{base}} = 0.50\%$. As $H \to 1.00$, the fee smoothly converges to $4 \times P_{\text{base}} = 2.00\%$. The borrower saves at least 300 to 800 basis points compared to liquidation, and collateral is preserved intact.

09 The Clamp-Only Policy Compiler & authorityHash

Mathematical Non-Escalation

The core security invariant of BULWARK is: Agents propose. Policy compiles. An AI agent, prompt injection, or compromised underwriter model can never increase its authorized capital or broaden its operating authority.

The Policy Compiler calculates the strict minimum intersection of six immutable boundary constraints:

Mathematical Intersection of Authority Limits
AuthorizedAmount ≤ min(AgentIntentAmount, GrantMaxAmount, BandCap(HF), PolicyMaxPerRescue, DailyVelocityBudgetRemaining, DeskAvailableCapacity)

Every compiled execution creates a cryptographically immutable authorityHash:

Cryptographic Authority Binding
authorityHash = keccak256(grantId || bandIndex || intentHash || authorizedAmount || nonce)

If any parameter is tampered with post-compilation, the hash mismatches and KeeperHub execution aborts fail-closed.

11 Security Invariants & Attack Hardening

Zero-Revert Discipline

BULWARK is hardened against both web3 smart contract attack vectors and autonomous LLM prompt vulnerabilities:

  • Adversarial Prompt Injection Bounding: LLM triage models are strictly advisory (AGENT OUTPUT). The LLM is only presented with a discrete set of pre-calculated feasible math plans. Even if an LLM is prompt-injected to output malicious numbers, the Policy Compiler clamps the intent to valid candidate boundaries.
  • Double-Spend & Re-entrancy Guard: Unique SHA-256 Idempotency-Key headers ensure network retries never replay transactions. Grant state transitions are atomic and fail-closed.
  • Zero Secret Leakage Invariant: Private keys never exist on disk, in memory, or in browser code. All on-chain mutations are signed inside KeeperHub's Turnkey enclaves. API keys are masked and never returned over public endpoints.
  • Simulation Footgun Guard: As identified in integration research, KeeperHub ignores simulate: true on protocol-action routes. BULWARK enforces client-side assertion checks (assertSimulationSafety) to prevent accidental live mempool broadcast.

13 Multi-Agent Dutch Auction Orderbook

Competitive Liquidity

Borrowers are not locked to a single underwriter. Multiple autonomous desks register with distinct risk models, balance sheets, and pricing strategies.

When a borrower position requires rescue, the Orderbook Engine matches demand against registered desks using three strict invariants:

  • Capacity Backing Invariant: A desk can never commit more capital than its verified on-chain wallet balance (reservedCapacity ≤ realBalance). Phantom liquidity is banned.
  • Linear Dutch Decay: Underwriting quotes begin at a ceiling and decay linearly over time until filled or expired.
  • Deterministic Tie-Breaking: Tied fee quotes are broken deterministically by verified historical reputation (successful rescues, zero reverts, desk age).
